Chris GibBot SMS Privacy Policy

This policy applies to SMS messages sent by Chris GibBot from a personal Twilio number for a hobby project.

Information collected

The mobile number you provide when you opt in, plus opt-in and opt-out records needed to honor your consent.

How it is used

That number is used only to send the Chris GibBot messages you consented to, such as project alerts and related notices, and to process STOP and HELP requests.

No sharing of mobile information

Mobile numbers and SMS opt-in information will not be shared, sold, or transferred to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Message frequency and fees

Message frequency varies, typically a few messages per week. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP to cancel. Reply HELP for help.

Contact

Questions about Chris GibBot SMS: use the contact email on the GitHub profile ylgibby.
